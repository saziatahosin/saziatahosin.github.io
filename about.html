I am a Master of Research (MRes) candidate at the University of Southern Queensland (UniSQ), Australia, supported by a fully funded research scholarship. I work on artificial intelligence and computer vision for medical and environmental imaging, from diagnosing disease in medical scans to monitoring drought from satellite images.

I completed my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering at Daffodil International University (DIU), Bangladesh. As a Research Associate at DIU’s Health Informatics Research Lab, I worked with Dr. Md Zahid Hasan (DIU) and Dr. Sami Azam (Charles Darwin University) on AI for tumour detection, disease classification and medical image analysis, collaborating with teams across several countries.

My undergraduate thesis, FedVGM, on federated learning with explainable AI across multiple medical image datasets, received an A+ and was published in the IEEE Journal of Biomedical and Health Informatics. I also review for journals including Scientific Reports (Nature Portfolio).

The same methods run through both areas: deep learning, transfer learning, explainable AI and privacy-preserving learning, applied to images at very different scales.

I believe that with care and the right application, AI can make healthcare and environmental management more accurate, accessible and equitable, especially for communities with the least access to specialist expertise.
